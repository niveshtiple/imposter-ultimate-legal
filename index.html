Effective Date: October 5, 2026

IMPOSTER: ULTIMATE (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is handled.

Game settings, preferences, statistics, progress, achievements, and other gameplay-related information may be stored locally on your device so the App can function properly.

If you purchase an IMPOSTER: ULTIMATE Pro subscription, purchase and subscription-related information may be processed by Google Play and our subscription service provider, RevenueCat, to verify purchases, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and provide Pro features.

We do not receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the email address you use and the contents of your message. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the App and its features.

  • Process and verify purchases and subscriptions.

  • Maintain, secure, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not sell personal information.
